How we work

Six clear steps. You always know what's happening and what you'll receive next.

  1. Discovery

    A call or visit to understand what you need.

    You get: A written quote, page plan and timeline

  2. Content

    We send a simple checklist for your text, photos and logo.

    You get: A content checklist (we can write and photograph for you too)

  3. Design

    We design your home page for phone and desktop.

    You get: A design preview, with revision rounds included

  4. Build

    We build every page and connect forms, WhatsApp and maps.

    You get: A private preview link to test on your own phone

  5. Launch

    We go live on your domain and check everything again.

    You get: Your live website and a launch post to share

  6. Handover & support

    We train you, hand over every login and stay on call.

    You get: A handover document, training and free support

Questions people ask

How long does a website take?

About 1 week for Starter, 2–3 weeks for Business and 3–5 weeks for an Online store. The clock starts once we have your content, and we agree the dates in your quote.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo, photos, contact details and a few lines about your business. We send a simple checklist. If you don't have photos or text, we can write and photograph for you as an add-on.

Who owns the website and domain?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, and at handover you receive every login and your content.

Are there yearly costs?

Yes. A domain and hosting renew every year. We tell you the exact renewal cost in your quote, so there are no surprises.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. Training is included, and the Online store package lets you add and edit products yourself. Or choose a care plan and we'll make changes for you.

How does payment work?

50% to start, 30% when you approve the design and 20% before launch. Your quote lists every item.

What happens after the free support ends?

Your site keeps running as normal. For ongoing changes, backups and renewals handled for you, you can choose a yearly care plan, or ask us for one-off changes whenever you need them.
